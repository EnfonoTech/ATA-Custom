Part A — Getting started

1. What the portal is

A simpler front door to the ERP, built around how ATA works day to day.

ATA keeps all its project information in ERPNext. ERPNext is a business system (an "ERP"). It is built on a framework called Frappe.

ERPNext has its own screens, called the Desk. The Desk address ends in /app. The Desk is powerful but complex. The portal is a simpler set of screens on top of the same data: projects, drawings, the standard folder tree, sharing and clients.

ERPNext DeskProject Portal
Addresshttps://<your-ERP-address>/apphttps://<your-ERP-address>/portal-app
Used forSetting things up and administrationDaily work
Typical usersSuper AdminsAll staff and client contacts
ExamplesRoles, users, settings, email set-up, audit listsProjects, files, sharing, tasks, clients

The portal does not keep a separate copy of your data. Every project, file, share, team and reminder is a normal ERPNext record. A change in the portal shows in the Desk, and the other way round.

It does this for youSo that
Builds the same folder tree for every projectAnyone who opens a project knows where a title deed or a permit is.
Keeps documents private by defaultOnly signed-in people with access can open them.
Gives each client their own doorA client sees only their own company's projects, never another client's.
Records who did whatEvery upload, share and deletion is saved against the person who did it. Opening or downloading a file is not recorded (the Desk's Access Log gets no entry), and the opens counter on guest links is not reliable (16.7).

Other ways in:

  • The ERPNext apps screen (/apps) has a Project Portal tile. It opens the portal. Every Desk user sees this tile, but only people with a portal role (Super Admin, Department Admin, Team Lead, Team Member or Client Customer) or a place on a project team get any data. A Desk user without one, who is already signed in to the Desk, is not signed out. They see an empty Projects list, and New project fails with "Not permitted". Ask a Super Admin to give you a role (ordinary staff are Team Members).
  • The Desk has a Project Portal workspace with shortcuts (see section 24).
  • There is also a Portal App page inside the Desk (/app/portal_app). The normal way in is still /portal-app.

2.2 Who sees which projects

This is the most important rule. You see the projects your role gives you, and no others.

RoleSees these projects
Super AdminEvery project.
Department AdminThe projects of the teams they administer, plus the projects that belong to no team yet (so somebody can put them under a team), plus the projects they are on or lead.
Team LeadThe projects of the team they lead, plus the projects they are on or lead. Never the projects that belong to no team.
Team MemberOnly the projects they are on or lead.
Client CustomerOnly the projects whose Customer is one of theirs.

A project belongs to a team when its Portal Team (7.5) is that team, or when somebody from that team is on its Team card or is its Lead Architect. A project that belongs to no team is seen by Department Admins, Super Admins and the people on it. A Team Lead does not see it unless they are on it or lead it.

  • A new Team Member sees no projects at first. They can sign in, but their Projects list is empty (it says "No projects match your filters."), and so are the Kanban board, Gantt chart, Calendar, Tasks, the Files hub and the search. They can still use Daily Task, Profile and Shared (files other people shared with them). A project appears for them as soon as somebody puts them on it: a Super Admin, a Department Admin, a Team Lead or the project's Lead Architect adds them to the project's Team card (7.6), or does it from the Org Chart with Assign to project… (19.3).
  • What you see, you can change. A staff member can edit, rename and delete the projects they see, move them on the Kanban board, upload to them and share from them. A client contact can open their projects but change nothing. Narrower rules apply to project money (2.5), to the Team card, the Portal Team and the client logins (2.3).
  • Being in a team is not the same as being on a project. For a Team Lead and a Department Admin, the team is their scope: it decides which projects they see. For a Team Member, being in a team opens no project. Only the project's own Team card does.
  • Being on any project team counts as portal access. A login on a project team can sign in and see that project, even without a portal role (it then counts as a Team Member). This includes someone added with Assign To on a Project in the Desk. Only put ATA staff on project teams. In the Desk, never assign a project to a client login or to any other outside person. The portal refuses client logins on a project team.
These rules are enforced in the portal onlyIn the ERPNext Desk, the standard Projects User role has wider rights over Projects than the portal gives (for example, ERPNext lets that role edit and delete a Project). Keep staff working in the portal, and give Desk access with care.

2.4 The Lead Architect (Portal Project Manager)

Each project can name one Lead Architect. In ERPNext this is the Portal Project Manager field on the Project. Being the Lead Architect gives four things:

  1. The project is yours. You see it and may change it, even when you are not on its Team card.
  2. Money (Department Admins). A Department Admin sees a project's value only when they are its Lead Architect.
  3. Team. The Lead Architect may add people to the project's Team card. They may not remove people. Only Super Admins and Department Admins can.
  4. Gantt grouping. The Lead Architect may set the project's Portal Team.

Rules for changing it:

  • A Super Admin may name anyone, or clear it.
  • A Department Admin may name anyone else, or clear it. They may not make themselves the Lead Architect of a project that somebody else leads. Ask a Super Admin.
  • A Team Lead or Team Member may only claim a project that has no Lead Architect. Once it names someone else, only a Super Admin or a Department Admin can change it.
  • Saving a project without changing its Lead Architect (for example in Edit Project, 6.5) does not count as a change, so anyone who can change the project can save it.
  • A client login can never be the Lead Architect.

In practice only Super Admins and Department Admins can pick a Lead Architect in the portal. The Lead Architect / Select Architect list is empty for Team Leads and Team Members, so they must ask an admin. For admins the list shows every enabled login (up to 200), client logins included. Never pick a client login.

2.5 Who sees money

The project's value is the ERPNext field Estimated Cost (shown in SAR).

PersonSees value on
Super AdminEvery project
Department AdminOnly projects where they are the Lead Architect
Everyone else (Team Leads, Team Members and clients)No project

This rule applies everywhere money appears: the Dashboard, the Projects list, the project page and ATA AI Chat. So two admins can correctly see different totals. The project's other money fields (for example billing totals and the margin) are hidden from the same people.

2.6 How ATA is set up today

At the October 2026 update, ATA's existing logins were carried over to the five roles:

  • ERPNext administrators are Super Admins.
  • People who held the old manager role became Department Admins of every team. Because they administer every team, they keep seeing and managing every project, as before. A Super Admin can narrow a Department Admin to fewer teams from the Org Chart.
  • People who already led a team became Team Leads.
  • Everyone else on staff is a Team Member. A Team Member now sees only the projects they are on. Before the update, every staff member could open every project. Until someone puts a Team Member on a project, their Projects list is empty.
  • Client contacts are Client Customers. They see only their own company, as before.

New here?If you have never signed in, set your password first (3.2).

3.2 First time: setting your password

  • You get a welcome email. Its subject is "Welcome to …" or "Complete Registration". Click the Complete Registration button and choose a password. Client contacts are then taken straight into the portal. Staff are usually taken to the ERPNext apps screen (/apps), or to the default app if one is set in System Settings. Click the Project Portal tile, or open /portal-app yourself.
  • You got no email. Some staff logins were created without a welcome email. Use Forgot Password? (next section) to set your first password.
The link in the email expiresOn the ATA site it lasts 72 hours. A Super Admin sets this in the Desk under System Settings → Reset Password Link Expiry Duration. If it has expired, use Forgot Password? or ask for a new link.

3.3 Forgot password

You can ask for a reset link yourself, on the portal's own sign-in screen. It does not send you to ERPNext's page, and nobody has to know your old password.

  1. Click Forgot Password?On the sign-in screen, next to Remember me. The same card now says Reset your password.
  2. Type your emailIn Email. If you had already typed your email in the sign-in box, it is filled in for you.
  3. Click Send reset linkA green message appears: "If that address belongs to a portal login, a reset link is on its way."
  4. Open the emailIt is called "Password Reset". Click Reset your password.
  5. Choose a new password

To go back without sending anything, click Back to sign in.

  • The answer is always the same. The portal never tells you whether the address has a login, so nobody can use the form to find out who has an account. If no email arrives, check how you spelled the address, then see section 28.
  • Only portal logins get a link. A disabled login, or an address that is not a portal login, gets no email, but sees the same green message.
  • The email is queued, like every portal email. It normally arrives within a few minutes.
  • The link has a lifetime. On the ATA site it works for 72 hours. A Super Admin sets this in System Settings (see 3.2). A newer link replaces the older one.
  • There is a limit. To stop misuse, the form accepts at most five requests an hour from the same internet address. After that, wait and try again.
  • Where you land. A client contact who chooses a new password is taken straight back into the portal. Staff go where ERPNext normally sends them after choosing a password (usually the apps screen, /apps): click the Project Portal tile, or open /portal-app yourself.
In ERPNextThe portal asks ERPNext for a normal password-reset link and queues ERPNext's "Password Reset" email. Choosing the new password then happens on ERPNext's own page.

Administrators can also send you a reset link (or, for a Super Admin, set a new password for you): see 3.8.

3.7 Notifications (the bell)

14.4 Dated upload folders and automatic names

The portal keeps every upload session in its own dated folder. This keeps history tidy. It applies to Upload files and Upload folder. Upload ZIP goes straight into the chosen folder, with no dated folder. So do a client's uploads, which go straight into Outgoing.

  • Each upload creates a new folder inside the folder you chose. Upload files: NN_YYYY-MM-DD, or NN_YYYY-MM-DD_<file name> for a single file. Upload folder: NN_YYYY-MM-DD_<folder name>.
  • NN is the next number after the folders already there. In a folder that already has 6 sub-folders, the first upload gets 07_.
  • If the same name already exists, _v2, _v3 and so on are added.
  • Upload files only: each file is renamed to <name>_<folder>_<date>.<extension>. Example: plan.pdf uploaded into 02-TITLE DEED becomes plan_02-TITLE-DEED_2026-09-30.pdf.
  • In the new name, spaces in the folder name become dashes. Signs such as & are dropped.
  • To use a different name, change the File name box before you upload. (Upload folder keeps the original names; see 14.5.)
  • The Date box changes only the file name. The dated folder always uses today's date.
  • If a file with exactly the same content is already stored on the site, ERPNext reuses that earlier file's name, so the name you typed may not be used.
  • If the name is already taken by another file on the site, ERPNext adds six characters before the extension (for example plan_02-TITLE-DEED_2026-09-30a1b2c3.pdf).

28. FAQ and troubleshooting

I still see the old screens after an update.

Reload the page. If that does not help, do a hard refresh: Ctrl + Shift + R (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + R (Mac). If it is still old, close every portal tab and open the portal again, or try a private window.

I cannot see a project I know exists.
  • A Team Member sees only the projects they are on. If the project is not in your list, ask a Super Admin, a Department Admin, a Team Lead or the project's Lead Architect to add you to its Team card (section 7.6).
  • A Team Lead or a Department Admin sees the projects of their teams (2.2). A project filed under another team, with nobody from your team on it, is not in your list. Only Department Admins see the projects that belong to no team.
  • If you see no project at all, your login may have no portal role. Ask a Super Admin to give you one (ordinary staff are Team Members).
  • Client contacts: the project needs its Customer set to your company, and your login must be linked to that customer. Ask your ATA contact.
  • You opened the portal from the Desk (the /apps tile) and the Projects list is empty: your login has no portal role. The portal does not sign you out in this case. Ask a Super Admin to give you a role.
  • You use the Active project picker on the Files hub, or the project lists on the File Browser or Contracts: these list only the 500 most recently changed projects. Find the project with the search on the Projects page instead.
Sign-in says "Sign-in did not keep a session (cookies blocked or wrong site URL). Check browser settings."

Check that your browser allows cookies for the site, and that you are using the portal address ATA gave you. If cookies are fine, your account may need an extra step that the portal's sign-in screen cannot show: a two-factor code, or a forced password change because your password has expired. Sign in once at https://<your-ERP-address>/login (ERPNext's own sign-in page), complete the step it asks for, then open /portal-app.

What do the sign-in error messages mean?
  • Invalid login credentials: the email or password is wrong. Use Forgot Password?.
  • User disabled or missing: your login is disabled. Ask a Super Admin.
  • Your account has been locked and will resume after N seconds: too many wrong passwords in a row (by default 10). Wait that long and try once more, or use Forgot Password?.
  • Server error. Please try again.: your login has no portal role (3.1).
  • Sign-in did not keep a session…: see the entry above.
A button I expected is missing.

Buttons appear only when your role allows the action (section 2.3). For example: Remove on a project's Team card is for Super Admins and Department Admins; Create team is for Super Admins; Dashboard and Routing rules are for admins; New Project shows only if you may create projects. Client contacts can open their projects but never change them. After a change of role, sign out and sign in again.

I am new and my Projects list is empty.

A new Team Member sees no projects until somebody adds them to a project's Team card (section 2.2). Ask your Team Lead, or an admin.

I took someone out of a team, but they still see projects.

Taking a person out of a team does not take them off projects. Remove them on each project's Team card (section 7.6).

Why can I see the project but not its value?

See section 2.5. Only Super Admins see all values. A Department Admin sees only projects where they are the Lead Architect. Team Leads and Team Members see no values.

The welcome or reset link has expired.

Links last 72 hours on the ATA site. Use Forgot Password?, or ask an admin to send you a new link (section 3.8).

The email did not arrive.
  • Check your spam or junk folder.
  • Emails are queued and can take a few minutes.
  • Forgot Password? shows the same message for every address, so it cannot tell you the address was wrong. Check how you spelled it and try again. The form accepts five requests an hour. Or ask an admin to send you a reset link (section 3.8).
  • If the screen said the email "could not be sent", no outgoing email account is set up. Tell a Super Admin.
  • A Super Admin can check the Email Queue list in the Desk.
  • If the Email Queue shows the email as Not Sent for more than a few minutes, the background scheduler may be off. Ask a Super Admin to have the developer switch it on. The hourly share expiry also depends on it.
The client cannot see the 06-CLIENT SUBMITTAL folder.

The portal finds the folder by its name, 06-CLIENT SUBMITTAL ("06 - CLIENT SUBMITTAL" with spaces is found too). See section 23.7.

A client opened a link to Tasks (or Kanban, Calendar, Gantt Chart, Daily Task, AI Chat) and landed on Projects.

That is expected. These pages are for ATA staff only, so the portal takes client contacts to Projects. Send clients links to their project page or their files instead.

The Team card says "Client contacts cannot be added to the project team".

A client login is in a User Group you added, or was already on the team from before. (The Add user search never offers client logins.) Clients get access through the project's Customer, on the Client Customer logins card (section 23). A Super Admin or a Department Admin must click Remove next to that person on the Team card.

New task says "Assign the task to an active ATA staff login."

The person in Assign to is disabled or is not an ATA staff login. Pick someone else, or leave the box empty (section 11.4).

My upload was refused.
  • The file type may be blocked (Appendix C), or the file may be too big.
  • Clients can only upload into 06-CLIENT SUBMITTAL / Outgoing ("You can upload only into the 06-CLIENT SUBMITTAL/Outgoing folder.").
  • For staff, Upload files and Upload ZIP are grey only when the project has no folders yet (see "A project made in the Desk has no folders" below). Otherwise the first folder is pre-selected, so always check Goes into <folder>.
  • A .zip above about 25 MB is refused by Upload ZIP (section 14.6).
A project made in the Desk has no folders.

The standard folders are built on the first portal upload.

  1. Open the Files hubPick the project in Active project.
  2. Click the Project folder (all files) card
  3. Click Use this folder for upload
  4. Upload one fileThe standard folders (Incoming and Outgoing included) are created.
  5. Reload the page

Your file is saved in a new dated folder at the top of the project (for example 07_2026-09-30_<name>), next to the new standard folders. Use a file that belongs there.

A new folder from the template is missing in an existing project.

Template changes reach only projects that had no folders. Existing projects keep their own folders.

The file went into a folder I did not expect.

Upload files and Upload folder put your files into a new dated folder inside the folder you chose (14.4). A routing rule, or the project page's own concept-studies copies (14.10), may also have placed a copy elsewhere. On the project page, changing File Classification or PDF Type can also change the destination folder (14.3).

A guest link stopped working.

It expired or was revoked. Create a new link (section 16.4).

Someone left my project team without anyone removing them.

Their Assign To on the project may have been closed or cancelled in the Desk. That removes them from the team. Add them again.

I get "Invalid Request" after signing in.

Reload the page once. The portal gets a fresh security token automatically. If it keeps happening, check that your browser allows cookies for the site.

"Maximum Attachment Limit … has been reached" when uploading.

Ask a Super Admin to have the developer run the update (bench migrate). It lifts the limit for projects.

"Set default Customer Group and Territory in Selling Settings, or create masters first."

No Customer Group or Territory exists on the site. A Super Admin creates at least one of each in the Desk, and ideally sets them as Default Customer Group and Default Territory in Selling Settings. Without those defaults, the portal uses the first group and territory.

"Set a default Company or pass company" / "Set a default Company first." (creating a project or a team)

A Super Admin sets the default Company in the Desk (Global Defaults).

"Daily Task is not set up on this site yet. Run bench migrate and try again."

Ask a Super Admin to have the developer run the update.

"Folder sharing is unavailable: this site has no encryption_key configured."

Tell a Super Admin. The developer must add the key to the site configuration. If the key is ever changed, every existing guest link stops working.

A client lands on a plain ERPNext page instead of the portal after signing in.

They can open /portal-app directly. A Super Admin should check that none of their roles has a Home Page set, and that Portal Settings → Default Portal Home is empty. Either of those overrides the portal's own redirect.

Something else is wrong.

See section 29.

29. Getting help

  1. Reload the pageThis clears most one-off problems.
  2. Sign out and sign in againThis fixes problems after a permission change.
  3. Report it with detailsTell an administrator what you were doing, which project or file, the exact message, and roughly what time. The time matters most, because it lets them find the entry in the Desk's Error Log.

  • "Portal: zip include <file ID>": a file was left out of a ZIP download.
